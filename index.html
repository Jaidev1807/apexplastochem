Custom Manufacturing

Your vision.
Our moulds.

If your product needs packaging that doesn't exist yet — we build it from scratch. Custom shapes, sizes, neck diameters, cap types, colours, and finishes.

01

Share Your Requirements

Tell us volume, shape, neck size, material, colour, and how the product will be filled and capped. Sketch or spec sheet both work.

02

Mould Design & Approval

Our engineering team creates a mould design. You review and approve before any tooling begins.

03

Sample Production

We produce a physical sample batch for your testing and regulatory approval. Adjustments are made before full production.

04

Full Scale Production

Once approved, we move to full production with consistent quality checks. Your mould is stored for repeat orders.
