Your packaging, built from scratch

We develop custom moulds to your exact specification: volume, shape, neck size, colour and cap type. A sketch or a spec sheet is enough to get started.

What we can customise

  • Bottle, jar and jerrycan shape and volume
  • Neck diameter and finish
  • Cap type and closure design
  • Colour, including brand colours
  • Premium finishes: metalizing and hot-foil stamping

How it works

  • 1. Share your requirements: volume, shape, neck size, material, colour, and how the product is filled and capped.
  • 2. Mould design and approval: our engineers design the mould, and you approve it before any tooling begins.
  • 3. Sample production: we produce a sample batch for your testing and regulatory approval.
  • 4. Full-scale production: consistent quality checks, and your mould is stored for repeat orders.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to start a custom project?
Tell us the volume, shape, neck size, material, colour, and how the product will be filled and capped. A sketch or spec sheet both work.
Do we approve the design before tooling?
Yes. Our engineering team creates a mould design, and you review and approve it before any tooling begins.
Will we get samples before full production?
Yes. We produce a physical sample batch for your testing and regulatory approval, and make adjustments before full production.
What happens to our mould afterwards?
Your mould is stored at our facility for repeat orders.
Do you export to Canada and the USA?
Yes. We supply customers internationally and have a Canadian contact number, +1 (437) 477-4287. Contact us with your volumes for a quote.

Have packaging in mind?

Send us a sketch or spec sheet, and we'll come back with a mould design and quote.
