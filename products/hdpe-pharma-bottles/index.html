Pharma Packaging

HDPE Pharma Bottles &
Tablet Containers

White HDPE packer bottles from 40cc to 950cc for tablets, capsules, vitamins and supplements. Made on injection and extrusion blow moulding machines at our ISO 9001 certified plant in Gujarat, India.

Request samples See size charts

Pharmaceutical-grade bottles, built to spec

Our HDPE packers are straight-sided, wide-mouth bottles designed for pills, tablets and capsules. HDPE gives an excellent moisture barrier, and every bottle is made in opaque white as standard, with custom colours on request.

Smaller sizes are made by injection blow moulding (IBM) in a clean room environment with minimal human contact, for consistent neck dimensions and a clean finish. Larger sizes are made by extrusion blow moulding (EBM).

Range at a glance

  • Sizes: 40cc to 950cc packers; 1000cc to 3000cc large rounds
  • Neck finishes: 33-400, 38-400, 45-400 and 53-400 continuous thread
  • Styles: round packers, Pharma Line packers and apothecary bottles
  • Material: HDPE, food and pharma grade
  • Process: IBM and EBM

Used for

  • Tablets, capsules and pills
  • Vitamins and nutritional supplements
  • Powders and granules (larger sizes take scoops)

Caps and closures to match

We also make the closures: standard screw caps, CPSC-compliant child-resistant caps for North American markets, and inner plugs for leak-proof sealing. See our caps range →

Frequently asked questions

What sizes of HDPE pharma bottles do you make?
Our stock range covers 40cc to 950cc packer bottles, plus large rounds from 1000cc to 3000cc. Custom sizes can be made with new moulds.
Which neck finishes are available?
Standard continuous-thread (C/T) neck finishes from 33-400 to 53-400, with larger 70, 100 and 110mm necks on large rounds.
Do you supply matching caps, including child-resistant closures?
Yes. We manufacture caps and closures, including CPSC-compliant child-resistant caps, plus inner plugs for leak-proof sealing.
Do you export to Canada and the USA?
Yes. We supply customers internationally and have a Canadian contact number, +1 (437) 477-4287. Contact us with your volumes for a quote.
Can you make a custom bottle?
Yes. We develop custom moulds for shape, volume, neck size, colour and finish, then produce samples for your approval before full production.
