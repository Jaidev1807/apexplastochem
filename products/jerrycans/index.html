HDPE Jerrycans
1 to 15 Litres

Heavy-duty jerrycans in HDPE and PP for edible oil, chemicals, pesticides and sanitizers, with a choice of handle and spout configurations.

Request samples See jerrycan styles

Strong containers for liquids

Our jerrycans are blow moulded in HDPE and PP for strength and chemical resistance. They are trusted by leading FMCG brands, including Vimal Oil for its edible oil range.

Range at a glance

  • Sizes: 1 litre to 15 litres
  • Material: HDPE and PP
  • Handles and spouts: multiple configurations, custom spouts available
  • Colours: any colour, including brand colours

Used for

  • Edible oil
  • Chemicals and pesticides
  • Sanitizers and cleaning products

Production capacity

With 6 blow moulding machines across 2 facilities, we can scale up quickly for high-volume orders.

What sizes of jerrycans do you make?
