Closures for every bottle

Our Alex Pharmapack division injection moulds a full range of dispensing closures for pharmaceutical, personal care, food and household packaging, from small 14mm caps to 63mm spice closures.

Cap types

  • FTC flip-top caps: 14mm to 28mm
  • Oval FTC caps: 15mm to 25mm
  • SSFT snap caps: 15mm to 25mm
  • Spice FTC caps: 38mm to 63mm
  • Press-stop caps: 19mm to 28mm
  • Two-fold FTC: 38mm

Inners and plugs

Bowl-type inner plugs, 28mm bottle plugs and plastic inner plugs in HDPE and LDPE give a leak-proof seal under the cap for pharmaceutical and food packaging.

Finishes

Any colour, plus metalizing and hot-foil stamping for brands that want their closures to stand out on the shelf.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes of flip-top caps do you make?
FTC flip-top caps from 14mm to 28mm, oval FTC and SSFT snap caps from 15mm to 25mm, spice caps from 38mm to 63mm, press-stop caps from 19mm to 28mm, and a 38mm two-fold FTC.
Can caps be made in our brand colour?
Yes. Caps can be moulded in any colour, with metalizing and hot-foil stamping available as premium finishes.
Do you supply inner plugs?
Yes. We make bowl-type inner plugs, 28mm bottle plugs and plastic inner plugs in HDPE and LDPE for leak-proof sealing.
Do you make child-resistant caps?
Yes. We make 25mm CPSC-compliant child-resistant caps in HDPE and PP.
Do you export to Canada and the USA?
Yes. We supply customers internationally and have a Canadian contact number, +1 (437) 477-4287. Contact us with your volumes for a quote.

Need caps for your bottles?

Tell us the neck size, cap type, colour and quantity, and we'll send samples and a quote.
