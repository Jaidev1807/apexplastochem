Safety closures for regulated markets

Child-resistant closures (CRCs) keep medicines and hazardous products out of children's hands. Ours meet the requirements of the US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), which makes them ready for North American pharmaceutical, supplement and cannabis brands.

Every closure is injection moulded by our Alex Pharmapack division and goes through rigorous protocol testing for safety compliance.

Options

  • Size: 25mm CRC (CPSC)
  • Material: HDPE and PP
  • Top designs: text debossed, embossed or pictorial
  • Finishes: any colour, plus metalizing and foiling

Used for

  • Prescription and over-the-counter medicines
  • Vitamins and nutritional supplements
  • Cannabis products

Frequently asked questions

Are your child-resistant caps CPSC compliant?
Yes. Our CRC closures are made to US Consumer Product Safety Commission standards and go through rigorous protocol testing for safety compliance.
What materials and sizes are available?
Our standard child-resistant cap is 25mm, made in HDPE or PP. Contact us if you need a different neck size.
Can the cap top carry our text or logo?
Yes. Tops are available with debossed text, embossed text or pictorial designs.
Do you make the bottles as well?
Yes. We make HDPE pharma packer bottles and tablet containers from 40cc to 950cc, so caps and bottles can come from one supplier.
Do you export to Canada and the USA?
Yes. We supply customers internationally and have a Canadian contact number, +1 (437) 477-4287. Contact us with your volumes for a quote.
